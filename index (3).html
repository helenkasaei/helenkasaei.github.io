Publications & Thesis

Master's Thesis

An Integrated Decision-Making Framework for Innovation and Product Strategy

Proposes a combined framework — built on the Innovation Matrix and the Ansoff Matrix, alongside SMART criteria and multi-criteria decision-making — for selecting and prioritizing innovation and product strategies under shifting market, technological, and customer conditions. Applied as a case study to the Entekhab Industrial Group.

Working Paper

An Integrated Framework for Explaining Innovation and Product Development Strategies Based on the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, and Kano Model

Brings the Innovation Matrix, the Ansoff Matrix, and the Kano Model together into one conceptual framework: the Kano Model surfaces which customer needs actually matter, the Ansoff Matrix turns that understanding into a growth direction, and the Innovation Matrix guides how new value gets created. The result is a structured way to move from "what do customers value" to "how should we grow" to "what should we build" — rather than treating each question in isolation.

Keywords — Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, Kano Model, Innovation Strategy, Product Development

Working Paper

From the Yakhchal to the Two-Door Refrigerator: Historical Foundations, Market Dynamics, and an Incremental Innovation Strategy for the Domestic Refrigeration Industry

Traces the domestic refrigerator's technology from ancient Persian yakhchal ice houses to today's two-door unit, then uses field surveys, e-commerce market data, and expert interviews to map where the industry actually sits on the technology–market innovation matrix and the Ansoff growth matrix. The conclusion: this is a mature, standardized platform where the winning strategy isn't radical redesign or new markets, but continuous incremental improvement and market penetration — competing through component quality, service, and financing rather than the underlying technology itself.

Keywords — Domestic refrigerators, Incremental innovation, Technology maturity, Technology–market innovation matrix, Ansoff matrix, Competitive positioning
