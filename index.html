About

My research centers on technology management, innovation strategy, and product innovation, with an emphasis on supporting strategic decision-making under technological and market uncertainty, including disruptive and radical innovation, product development, technology strategy, and multi-criteria decision-making, with the broader aim of helping organizations act on the innovation opportunities that matter most.

Publications & Thesis

Master's Thesis

An Integrated Decision-Making Framework for Innovation and Product Strategy

Proposes a combined framework, built on the Innovation Matrix and the Ansoff Matrix, alongside SMART criteria and multi-criteria decision-making, for selecting and prioritizing innovation and product strategies under shifting market, technological, and customer conditions. Applied as a case study to the Entekhab Industrial Group.

Working Paper

An Integrated Framework for Explaining Innovation and Product Development Strategies Based on the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, and Kano Model

Brings the Innovation Matrix, the Ansoff Matrix, and the Kano Model together into one conceptual framework: the Kano Model surfaces which customer needs actually matter, the Ansoff Matrix turns that understanding into a growth direction, and the Innovation Matrix guides how new value gets created.

Working Paper

From the Yakhchal to the Two-Door Refrigerator: Historical Foundations, Market Dynamics, and an Incremental Innovation Strategy for the Domestic Refrigeration Industry

Traces the domestic refrigerator's technology from ancient Persian yakhchal ice houses to today's two-door unit, then uses field surveys and market data to position the industry on the technology–market innovation matrix and the Ansoff growth matrix, concluding that incremental improvement and market penetration, not radical redesign, is the appropriate strategy.

Incremental Innovation · Technology Maturity · Competitive Positioning
