Master’s Thesis

Thesis in progressTechnology Management (Innovation)

An Integrated Decision-Making Framework for Innovation and Product Strategy

This thesis develops an integrated framework to support innovation and product strategy decisions under changing market, technological, and customer conditions. It brings together the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, Kano model, SMART criteria, and multi-criteria decision-making methods.

Case study: Entekhab Industrial Group (Snowa)

Supervisor: Professor Arash Shahin · PhD in Quality Engineering, Newcastle University, UK.

An Integrated Framework for Explaining Innovation and Product Development Strategies Based on the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, and Kano Model

The Kano Model helps identify customer needs that matter; the Ansoff Matrix helps select a growth direction; and the Innovation Matrix guides how new value can be created. The framework connects these perspectives to explain product and innovation strategy choices.

From the Yakhchal to the Two-Door Refrigerator: Historical Foundations, Market Dynamics, and an Incremental Innovation Strategy for the Domestic Refrigeration Industry

This paper examines the development of Iran’s domestic refrigeration industry, from traditional yakhchals to modern refrigerators, and considers market dynamics, incremental innovation, and industry strategy.
