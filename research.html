Master’s Thesis

An Integrated Decision-Making Framework for Innovation and Product Strategy

This master’s thesis develops an integrated framework for selecting and prioritising innovation and product strategies under changing market, technological, and customer conditions. It combines the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, Kano model, SMART criteria, and multi-criteria decision-making methods.

Case study: Entekhab Industrial Group (Snowa). Thesis in progress.

Supervisor: Professor Arash Shahin.

Working Papers

An Integrated Framework for Explaining Innovation and Product Development Strategies Based on the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, and Kano Model

This paper brings the Innovation Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, and Kano model together: Kano helps identify the customer needs that matter, Ansoff helps select a growth direction, and the Innovation Matrix guides how new value is created.

From the Yakhchal to the Two-Door Refrigerator: Historical Foundations, Market Dynamics, and an Incremental Innovation Strategy for the Domestic Refrigeration Industry

This paper traces the domestic refrigerator industry from traditional yakhchals to modern two-door refrigerators. It uses field surveys and market data to examine industry development, market dynamics, incremental innovation, and growth strategy.
